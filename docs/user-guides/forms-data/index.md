# Forms & Data

Ask commenters for more information, show your own data beside each comment, and check or track what happens in the thread.

## [Ask Commenters for More with Custom Fields](docs:user-guides/forms-data/ask-commenters-for-more-with-custom-fields)

Add Craft custom fields to the comment form, such as a star rating, a photo or a private order number. You choose for each field whether visitors fill it in and whether it's shown on the comment, with no templates to write.

## [Add Your Own Data to Comments](docs:user-guides/forms-data/add-your-own-data-to-comments)

Show extra information beside each comment that commenters don't enter themselves. The example adds a **Verified buyer** badge and the product size to reviews on a Craft Commerce shop.

## [Validate and Track Comments](docs:user-guides/forms-data/validate-and-track-comments)

Use the thread's browser events to check your own rules before a comment is sent, such as a minimum length, and to send comments, votes, follows and reports to Google Tag Manager.
